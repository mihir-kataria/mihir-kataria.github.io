  1. Acronyms

This page shows the one term chosen for each AIAG concept, so every page uses the same words. It's for anyone writing or editing the AIAG docs.

UseDon't useMeaning and notes
AIAGASU GPT, ASUGPT, GPT BotThe product. Define once per page as "AIAG (ASU Instructor Assistant GPT)" where needed. "The bot" is acceptable in the student guide.
admin paneladmin control panel, instructor control panelThe instructor pages for connecting a course and choosing materials.
chatthread, conversation, chat sessionOne saved sequence of questions and answers in the sidebar.
teaching staffcourse staff, instructional team, moderatorsInstructors and TAs together. Use "instructor" when only instructors can do something. In the student guide, say "your instructor or TA".
knowledge basevector database (as a concept), datasetAll the content AIAG can retrieve for one course.
passagematch, engine resultOne retrieved item: a discussion post, a reply, or a document chunk.
sourcescitations, referencesThe passages shown to students in the Source tab.
relevance checkrelevance filter, classifierThe LLM call that decides whether a question is about the course.
escalationHITL post, ask a humanPosting an unanswered question to the course discussion board. In technical docs, define "human-in-the-loop (HITL)" once. In the student guide, say "post your question to the discussion board".
discussion boarddiscussion forumCanvas Discussions or Ed Discussion.
Chinese, MandarinSimplified Chinese, zh (in prose)Use "Chinese" for the language setting and written text. Use "Mandarin" only for spoken voice input.
