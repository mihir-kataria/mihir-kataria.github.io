AIAG is your course's AI teaching assistant. It answers questions from your course's own materials and shows you where each answer came from. This page shows you how to ask a question, check the answer, change the language, use your voice, and get help from a person when AIAG can't answer.

To start, open AIAG from your course menu in Canvas. You don't need a separate login.

§ 01Ask a question

  1. Select the box that says Enter a question....
  2. Type your question.
  3. Select the send button, which looks like a paper airplane.

While AIAG works, you see Typing.... The answer then appears under your question.

You can ask about course content, assignments, course policies, or how to use AIAG. AIAG doesn't answer questions unrelated to your course. It also doesn't give exam dates or deadlines, so check your syllabus or Canvas for those.

§ 02Read the answer and its sources

Each answer has two tabs:

  • Response shows AIAG's answer.
  • Source shows the course material AIAG used, such as past discussion posts and their replies, with the date of each one.

Open Source when you want to check an answer or read the original posts.

§ 03Change the language

At the top of the page, open the language menu and choose English or 中文 (Chinese). Menus and buttons switch language right away.

AIAG answers in the language you ask in.

§ 04Ask with your voice

  1. Select the microphone button at the left of the question box.
  2. If your browser asks to use your microphone, allow it.
  3. Say your question. If the language is set to 中文, speak Mandarin.
  4. Select the microphone button again to stop. Your words appear in the question box.
  5. Check the text, fix any mistakes, and then send it.

If you see Please enable microphone access to use this feature., turn on microphone access for this site in your browser settings and try again.

§ 05Post a question AIAG can't answer

When AIAG can't find an answer in your course materials, it offers to post your question to the course discussion board, where your instructor or TA can answer it.

  1. In the Would you like to ask a human? window, select Confirm. To skip posting, select Cancel.
  2. In the Create New Discussion window, type a short title in Topic Title.
  3. Check the box under the title. Your question is already there, and you can edit it.
  4. Select Submit. You can't select Submit until both boxes have text.

A message at the top of the chat confirms that your question was posted. Check the course discussion board for an answer from your instructor or TA.
